## Reading the columns

**Acting hand** is whichever hand travelled furthest, which is the one holding the object. This matters more than the overall percentage, because a recording can track the resting hand perfectly and still be useless.

**Hand tracking** gives the fraction of samples where the acting hand was found, followed by the worst gap *inside* the tracked stretch. Samples missing before the hand first appears or after it last appears cost us nothing. A gap in the middle breaks the demonstration.

**Pose** counts the device poses passing both quality flags. Rejections in the first 2 seconds are VIO working out where it is, which the still period at the start of each recording covers.

**Head** is how far the glasses themselves travelled. It separates working at a bench from walking across a room. The September recording shows why that distinction matters.
